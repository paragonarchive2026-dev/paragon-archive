📜 Community Guidelines

  • Be human, be kind. No harassment, hate, or personal attacks — moderators can hide or remove posts with a written reason.
  • No spam or ads. Promotion belongs to the official sponsored-notice system, never the board.
  • Stay honest. Don't invent claims about Paragon products; real experiences only.
  • Report, don't fight. Use the 🚩 report button — every report reaches the real moderation desk.
  • Ideas welcome. Feature wishes belong on the Ideas board and genuinely reach the team's Suggestions desk.
