How it works

Three honest steps — no account wall

1
I

Pick a cabinet

Every cabinet prints its full rules on the Rules card inside the game, so you never play a rule you haven't read.

2
II

Play a seeded round

Delays, shuffles, sweeps, patterns and targets all come from a seeded draw. The seed is printed on your result so a game can be replayed exactly.

3
III

Pause any time

Close the tab mid-game and the Arcade offers to resume. Memory resumes exactly; timed rounds restart their current round — stated on each cabinet.
