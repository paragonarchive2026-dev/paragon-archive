The Story

Where It All Started

On August 1, 2026 one idea changed everything.

It started simply — a desire to build. Not just one website but many. A growing list of tools, platforms and experiences that people actually needed.

But as the list grew a new question emerged: how do people find all of this?

How does someone know where to go for the right tool, the right resource, the right experience — without wasting time, filling their phone with apps or fighting through the noise of the internet?

The answer became Paragon Archive.

Not a search engine. Not a directory. Something entirely different — a handcrafted collection of free, high-quality websites all built by the same team, all living in one place, all accessible with a single account.

One home. No stress. No storage problems. No cost. Just everything you need, right here.

That was the vision on day one. It still is today.

1. Who We Are

Paragon Archive is a product of Paragon, a tech brand founded and led by a passionate team based in Nigeria. We build free, accessible web tools for everyone on the planet. You can reach us at any time at paragon.archive.2026@gmail.com — just change the subject line depending on what you need to talk about.

2. What Data We Collect

When you use Paragon Archive we collect the following information to make your experience work properly and improve over time:

Account Information

  • Your name or username
  • Your email address
  • Your password (stored securely, never readable by anyone including us)
  • Your profile picture if you choose to upload one
  • The date you created your account

Usage Data

  • Which websites inside the archive you visit and when
  • How long you spend on each website
  • Websites you save, bookmark or add to collections
  • Reviews and ratings you write
  • Website requests you submit
  • Support messages, bug reports and optional screenshots you submit
  • Your recently visited history
  • Your recent search phrases, stored on this device or temporarily in Guest mode
  • Your achievements and stats

Device and Technical Data

Our hosting, authentication and security providers may process technical request data needed to deliver and protect the service:

  • Your browser type and version
  • Your device type (mobile, tablet, desktop)
  • Your operating system
  • Your IP address in infrastructure/security logs
  • Approximate country or city derived by a provider, not your exact address
  • Pages requested and basic navigation events only when the relevant service is active and permitted

Cookie Data

  • Essential storage that keeps you logged in and makes the site function
  • Optional tracking cookies only if a future tracking integration is activated and you consent

Analytics Data

  • Google Analytics is not currently connected
  • If it is activated later, it may collect consented aggregate traffic patterns, popular sections and general usage so we can improve the Archive

3. What We Do With Your Data

We use your data for these purposes only:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To personalize your experience (recently visited, recommendations, saved sites)
  • To remember your preferences (dark mode, notification settings)
  • To show you relevant content inside the archive
  • To improve Paragon Archive based on how people use it
  • To respond to your support requests, bug reports and feedback
  • To send you notifications about saved-website updates if you opt in and the production notification service is activated
  • To process your website requests and notify you if your requested website gets built

4. Advertisements

Paragon Archive does not currently load an advertising service. We may apply for a consent-aware ad service later to help keep the platform free. If ads are activated:

  • You will be given control through the available consent and ad preferences
  • Ads will be clearly labeled so you know what is an ad and what is Paragon content
  • We will not sell your personal information to advertisers
  • Any permitted targeting will use consented general category signals rather than a hidden Paragon identity profile
  • You will be able to manage the available ad preferences in Account settings

5. Cookies

Essential Cookies

These are required for the website to work. Without them you cannot log in, stay logged in or use core features. You cannot opt out of essential cookies and still use the site.

Tracking Cookies

No tracking-cookie integration is currently connected. If one is activated later, it may measure pages visited, time spent and navigation patterns only after the relevant consent. You can keep tracking disabled in Privacy Controls and the core site will still work.

A cookie notice will appear on your first visit giving you the option to accept or manage your cookie preferences.

6. Google Analytics

Google Analytics is not currently connected. If we activate it later, it must first respect your Analytics Tracking preference and will be used for aggregate usage statistics rather than to expose your Paragon account identity. You can keep Analytics Tracking turned off under Privacy Controls.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not trade your data. We do not give your data to anyone for their own marketing purposes. Period.

The only situations where your data may be seen by others:

  • Email delivery providers receive the destination email address and message content only to deliver requested authentication or transactional messages
  • Google Analytics may receive consented aggregate usage data only if the future integration is activated
  • Google Ads or AdSense may receive consented ad-delivery/category signals only if Paragon is approved and the future integration is activated
  • Legal requirement — if a valid legal authority in Nigeria requires us to disclose data we will comply with Nigerian law and notify you where legally permitted to do so

8. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep account data while your account is active and retain support/request records only as needed to operate, secure and improve the service. Secure account deletion and automated 30-day purging are not yet activated. Until that workflow exists, use the Delete Account control for current status and contact Privacy support with a deletion request.

9. Your Rights and Controls

You are in full control of your data on Paragon Archive. Here is what you can do at any time:

Delete Your Account

Go to Account, then Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Delete Account. The current control explains that secure deletion processing is pending and directs you to Privacy support; it does not falsely claim immediate deletion.

Download Your Data

Go to Account, then Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Download My Data. You will receive a file containing the account/browser data currently available to the Archive, excluding passwords and authentication tokens.

Opt Out of Analytics

Go to Account, then Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Privacy Controls, then Turn off Analytics Tracking.

Manage Cookies

Go to Account, then Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Cookie Preferences.

Manage Ad Preferences

Go to Account, then Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Ad Preferences.

Edit or Update Your Information

Profile editing is planned for Account settings. Until it is activated, contact Support if account information needs correction.

Privacy Controls

Essential authentication/site storage always remains enabled. Optional integrations must respect these choices.

10. Account Security

HTTPS Encryption

The production Archive and every submission/authentication endpoint must be served over HTTPS so data is encrypted in transit. Do not submit sensitive information through an unencrypted local or preview address.

Password Security

Your password is never stored in plain text. It is hashed and encrypted before storage meaning even our team cannot see what your password is.

Your Responsibility

Please use a strong password and keep it safe. Do not share your account with others. If you believe your account has been compromised contact us immediately at paragon.archive.2026@gmail.com with the subject line Account Security.

Paragon Archive is open to users of all ages. We do not specifically target children but we do not restrict access by age either. Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise younger users. We do not knowingly collect data from children in a way that would harm them and if you believe a child's data has been collected inappropriately please contact us immediately.

Paragon Archive is based in Nigeria. If you are accessing the archive from outside Nigeria your data may be processed under Nigerian standards. We do our best to align with international privacy principles including the spirit of GDPR and similar frameworks even though we are not legally bound by them as a Nigerian-based platform. We believe good privacy practices are universal.

Reporting a Bug

A bug is when something on Paragon Archive does not work the way it is supposed to. Before you report please read this so we can fix it as fast as possible:

What counts as a bug:

  • A page that will not load or crashes
  • A button that does not do anything when clicked
  • A website inside the archive that breaks or shows an error
  • Your account data not saving correctly
  • Features that are broken or behaving unexpectedly

What is NOT a bug:

  • A website you want that does not exist yet — use Request a Website instead
  • A design choice you disagree with
  • Slow internet on your end
  • A feature you think should work differently — use Feature Suggestion instead

When reporting a bug please include:

  • What you were doing when it happened
  • What device and browser you were using
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • A screenshot if possible — this saves us a lot of time

Use the contact form above and select Bug Report as your topic. Attach your screenshot directly in the form.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

🔑 Account

How do I create an account?

Tap the Account tab at the bottom of the screen. You will see options to sign up with Google or with your email. Follow the steps and your account will be ready in under a minute.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the login screen tap Forgot Password and enter your email address. We will send you a link to reset your password. Check your spam folder if you do not see it within a few minutes.

Can I use Paragon Archive without an account?

Yes. You can browse and open all websites as a Guest. Guest saves, reviews, collections, history and progress are temporary. A Guest session ends after 30 continuous minutes away or offline, or immediately when you choose End Guest Session. If you sign in or create an account before that, the live Guest activity merges into your account.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Account, then Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Delete Account. Secure permanent deletion still requires the planned backend deletion workflow, so the current control explains the status instead of claiming your account was deleted. You can contact Privacy support while that workflow is pending.

Can I download my data?

Yes. Go to Account, then Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Download My Data and we will prepare a file with the data currently available to your browser and account. Passwords and authentication tokens are excluded.

🌐 Websites

How do I open a website?

Find the website on the browse page, tap it to open the detail page and then tap the OPEN button. When the website allows framing it loads inside Paragon Archive. If the website blocks embedded previews, use Open in New Tab.

Why is a website not loading inside the archive?

This is usually temporary or the website may block embedded previews through its security policy. Try refreshing, closing and reopening it, or use Open in New Tab. If the problem continues report it with the Bug Report form above, including the website and your browser.

How do I save or bookmark a website?

Open the website detail page and tap the Bookmark action. Authenticated saves appear under Saved & Bookmarked in your Account tab and sync after Supabase activation. Guest saves last only for the current browser session.

How do I write a review?

Open the website detail page and scroll to Ratings & Reviews. Tap Write a Review, choose your stars, write your thoughts and submit. Authenticated reviews can sync to your account; Guest reviews are temporary for the session.

How do I request a website that does not exist yet?

Go to Account, then Settings, then Request a Website or open the Request a Website page from the menu. Sign in, fill in your idea and submit it. Each account can submit one request in every rolling seven-day period.

🔔 Notifications and Settings

How do I turn on notifications for a website I like?

Save the website first, then go to Account, then Settings, then Notifications. Saved website updates are highlighted in the Updates tab for authenticated users. Optional email delivery will become available after the production notification service is activated.

How do I switch between dark mode and light mode?

Go to Account, then Settings, then Dark Mode toggle. You can switch between dark and light mode at any time. The site defaults to dark mode.

How do I opt out of analytics tracking?

Go to Account, then Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Privacy Controls and turn off Analytics Tracking. Analytics scripts are not currently connected in this export.

💰 Pricing

Is Paragon Archive free?

Yes. Paragon Archive and every Paragon-built website are intended to remain completely free, and creating an account is free. Future approved Deployed websites may offer clearly labelled premium features under the Deployed rules; those transactions are not handled by Paragon.

Will it always be free?

That is the plan and the promise. Paragon exists to make things free and easy for everyone. The future plan is to keep the lights on through optional ads that you control. No advertising scripts are currently connected, and any future integration must respect your consent choices.

Paragon Archive is a free web-based platform presenting a curated collection of websites built by Paragon. The Deployed category is an approved future path for reviewed third-party websites; no public third-party deployment service is live in this build.

Paragon-built websites are intended to remain free to use. Future approved third-party websites may include free and premium content at the discretion of their creators, subject to the transparency rules below.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website may store on your device. Paragon Archive also uses browser storage such as localStorage and sessionStorage for preferences, authenticated-session data, temporary Guest activity, and PWA operation. The exact storage used depends on the feature.

✓

Essential Storage

Required to keep the current application working: authentication session data after Supabase activation, cross-page state, bookmarks/progress according to identity mode, theme, consent choices, security/session values, and PWA caching. Essential operation cannot be fully disabled while using account features.

Current: Used where required
○

Analytics Cookies

Future aggregate measurements may cover pages visited, time spent, navigation patterns, popular websites, and general device/browser data. Google Analytics is not connected now and must respect ParagonPrivacy.analyticsAllowed() before future loading.

Current: Not connected
○

Tracking & Advertising

Future consented systems could use general interests, ad interaction, or content preferences. Tracking cookies and Google Ads/AdSense are not connected and must respect tracking/ad consent before loading.

Current: Not connected

Your Cookie Choices

  • Accept All — records consent for all categories, but does not activate services that have not been integrated.
  • Essential Only — keeps optional Analytics, tracking, and ads disabled.
  • Manage Preferences — opens detailed controls.

Change your choices at any time through Account, then Settings, then Privacy & Security, or open the Privacy Controls page.
