Trips

Plan itineraries and budgets. This app does not book flights or hotels.

Boundary: no bookings, no live flight/hotel APIs — planning only. Pacing rule: max ~2–3 major stops per day; leave buffer for food and transit.
Entry requirements are checklists only — always confirm on official government sites for your passport.
Flights & hotels — planning standards (no booking)
  • Search flexible ±3 days; midweek often cheaper
  • Prefer one stop max for long-haul unless budget forces more
  • Check baggage + total duration, not price alone
  • Hotels: location over stars; walk-score to your day clusters
  • Book free-cancellation when plans are soft
  • This app does not search live inventory or take payment
